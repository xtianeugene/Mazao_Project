How to Pay with M-Pesa

  1. Click "Make New Payment" to start a payment transaction
  2. Enter your details including phone number (format: 2547XXXXXXXX) and amount
  3. Confirm on your phone - You'll receive an M-Pesa prompt on your mobile
  4. Enter your PIN to authorize the payment
  5. Wait for confirmation - You'll see a success message once complete
  6. Check status anytime using your checkout request ID

Important Notes:

  • Ensure you have sufficient funds in your M-Pesa account
  • Keep your checkout ID for future reference
  • Payments are processed in real-time
  • Contact support if you encounter any issues
