About Mazao

Our Mission

Mazao (which means "produce" in Swahili) is dedicated to connecting farmers directly with buyers, eliminating middlemen and ensuring fair prices for both producers and consumers.

How It Works

  1. Farmers list their produce - Set prices and quantities
  2. Buyers browse and order - Search for fresh produce
  3. Secure M-Pesa payments - Quick and secure transactions
  4. Delivery coordination - Get your produce delivered

Benefits

For Farmers:
  • Better prices for your produce
  • Direct access to customers
  • Secure and timely payments
  • Expanded market reach
For Buyers:
  • Fresh, quality produce
  • Competitive prices
  • Convenient M-Pesa payments
  • Direct from farm freshness
